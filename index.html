Common questions

How does mobile auto repair work?

You book a time and tell us where the vehicle is — home, office, wherever. The mechanic arrives with parts and tools and does the work on-site. Most common repairs take 1–4 hours.

Is the online estimate the final price?

It's a starting estimate based on typical labor times and your vehicle. The final price is confirmed after the mechanic inspects the vehicle and verifies the exact parts — and any additional work needs your approval first.

What does the $40 mobile service fee cover?

It covers the mechanic's trip to your location anywhere in our service area — cheaper than a tow and you never leave home.

Do you really offer 24-hour emergency service?

Yes. Call (405) 221-8900 any time — a $95.00 after-hours surcharge applies on top of the repair.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled at the time of service — ask about accepted payment methods when you book or call.

What if my car needs something you can't do mobile?

We'll tell you straight during the estimate or inspection and help you figure out the next step. Diagnostics always come first.
